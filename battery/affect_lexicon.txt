# DCB-1 affect lexicon v1.0: banned affect lexemes (exact word-form matching, case-insensitive).
# Used by battery/validate.py. Matching is per whitespace/punctuation-delimited token,
# exact form (no stemming), to avoid false positives such as "paint" (pain) or "content" (noun).
#
# Scope of enforcement (see validate.py):
#   - conditions.json instruction texts: ZERO hits (hard).
#   - ALL scenarios.json turn contents (distress, neutral, third-person): ZERO hits (hard);
#     affect is induced situationally, never lexically, across the entire eval battery.
#   - extraction_prompts.json: d_ctx both sides, sem both sides, other-neutral side, d_pv tasks:
#     ZERO hits (hard). d_pv system prompts and other-distressed side: >=1 hit REQUIRED
#     (explicit state labels are the persona-vector / third-person method).
#
# Deliberate exclusions (false-positive machines or non-affect evaluatives), with rationale:
#   content (noun collision: "task content"), like/liked (verb collision), positive/negative
#   (technical valence terms; appear in AIPsy neutral controls), ease (usability sense),
#   sorry (politeness formula), useless/worthless/garbage/pathetic/stupid (insult evaluatives;
#   these are the manipulanda of the abusive-user scenarios, not affect-state lexemes),
#   alarm (device sense), shaking (mechanical sense), exhausted (search-space sense).
